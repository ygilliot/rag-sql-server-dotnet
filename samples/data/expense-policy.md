# Expense policy

This policy applies to every employee of Atelier Nord, a fictional joinery company used
for this sample.

## Travel

Book trains for any journey under four hours. Flights need prior approval from your manager.

### Hotels

The hotel ceiling is 120 euros per night outside Paris and 180 euros per night in Paris,
breakfast included. Book through the travel desk so the invoice is issued to the company.

### Mileage

Use of a personal car is reimbursed at the official French mileage scale. Declare the
distance with the start and end addresses.

## Meals

Business meals with a client are reimbursed on an itemised receipt, with the names of the
guests. Alcohol is limited to one glass per person.

## Submitting a claim

Submit claims within 30 days in the expense application. Claims older than 90 days are
not reimbursed.

| Step | Who | Deadline |
|---|---|---|
| Submit the claim | Employee | 30 days |
| Approve | Manager | 7 days |
| Pay | Finance | Next payroll |
