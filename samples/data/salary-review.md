# Salary review process

Confidential: human resources only. Fictional content for this sample.

## Calendar

The annual salary review opens on 1 March. Managers submit proposals by 31 March and
human resources confirms the salary increases by 30 April.

## Salary bands

| Band | Role | Salary range |
|---|---|---|
| B1 | Apprentice joiner | 22,000 to 26,000 euros |
| B2 | Joiner | 28,000 to 36,000 euros |
| B3 | Workshop lead | 38,000 to 48,000 euros |

## Budget

The salary increase budget is set at 3 percent of the payroll, with a maximum individual
increase of 8 percent outside promotions.
