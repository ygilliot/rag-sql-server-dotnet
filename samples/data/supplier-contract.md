# Supplier contract template

Purchasing and legal teams only. Fictional content for this sample.

## Clause 4: Payment terms

Invoices are paid 45 days end of month from the invoice date. Late payment penalties apply
at three times the legal interest rate, plus the fixed recovery fee of 40 euros.

## Clause 7: Liability

The supplier's liability is capped at the total amount paid under the contract during the
previous twelve months, except for gross negligence, wilful misconduct and personal injury.

## Clause 9: Termination

Either party may terminate with three months' notice by registered letter. Orders already
confirmed remain due.
